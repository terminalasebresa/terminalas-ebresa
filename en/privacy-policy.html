We retain request and correspondence data for as long as necessary to process the request and pursue possible cooperation, and afterwards for up to a further 2 years, unless a longer period is required by applicable law (e.g. accounting requirements, if the data becomes part of a contract or accounting records). Once the retention period ends, the data is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate data;
  • request erasure of data ("the right to be forgotten"), where legally possible;
  • restrict processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, contact us at lukas@ebresa.lt. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (VDAI), vdai.lrv.lt, if you believe your data is being processed in breach of GDPR requirements.

7. Data security

We implement reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss or alteration. Only employees who need it to perform their duties have access to the data.
