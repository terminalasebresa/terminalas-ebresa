Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-10

This policy explains what cookies are, which cookies the UAB Ebresa website ebresa.lt uses, and how you can manage your choices.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that your browser stores on your device when you visit a website. Cookies help the website function properly, remember your preferences, or (with your consent) help us understand how visitors use the site.

2. How we manage consent

The first time you visit the website, a cookie notice is shown at the bottom of the page, where you can:

  • Accept all — both necessary and analytics cookies are enabled;
  • Keep necessary only — analytics cookies are not enabled;
  • Choose settings — enable or disable each cookie category individually.

You can change your choice at any time by clicking "Cookie settings" in the footer of the website.

3. Cookie categories

3.1 Necessary cookies

These cookies (more precisely, browser local storage) are required for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled.

NamePurposeDuration
ebresa_cookie_consentRemembers your chosen cookie settings so the notice isn't shown again each timeUntil you clear your browser data

3.2 Analytics cookies

Would help us understand how visitors use the website (e.g. which pages are most visited), using the Google Analytics service (provider — Google Ireland Limited).

Google Analytics cookies are only enabled once you give your consent — by clicking "Accept all" or turning on analytics cookies separately in the settings. If you don't give consent, or choose "Necessary only", Google Analytics does not run and these cookies are not used.

NamePurposeDurationProvider
_ga, _ga_*Distinguishing visitors and website usage statisticsUp to 2 yearsGoogle Ireland Limited

4. Third-party services

The request form is technically processed through a third-party service (FormSubmit), which may use its own technical cookies to secure form submission (e.g. protection against automated spam). This is not related to marketing or tracking purposes.

5. How to manage cookies in your browser

In addition to the settings provided on the website, you can also manage cookies in your web browser's settings — blocking them, deleting them, or being notified before they are accepted. Please note that fully disabling necessary cookies (browser storage) may cause some website functions to work incorrectly.

6. Related documents

For more on how we process your personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
