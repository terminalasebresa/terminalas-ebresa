EBRESA Terminal · Kybartai

Loading, storage and packing at our terminal in Kybartai

UAB Ebresa serves large wholesale clients in the agricultural and fertilizer sectors: wagon and truck loading, storage of bulk and packaged products, big-bag packing, fertilizer blending and forwarding — all in one location, right on Lithuania's border with the Kaliningrad region and ~40 km from Poland.

Services

Services built around every stage of your cargo

From cargo receiving and weighing to packing and forwarding — every service is tailored to your needs.

Mobile bulk product packing

We pack bulk products on-site using mobile equipment — throughput of around 700 tonnes per shift.

Wagon and truck loading

Fast, mechanised loading, unloading and transloading directly between wagon and truck, or vice versa.

Weighing

Truck weighing on the terminal's scales, with documented weight records.

Wagon storage

Secure storage of loaded or empty wagons on the terminal grounds.

Forwarding

Cargo receiving and dispatch — full coordination of documentation and logistics.

Storage of bulk and packaged products

Indoor space (50–1000 m²) and open-air storage areas for bulk, packaged and big-bag products.

Liquid product handling

Receiving, repackaging and safe storage of liquid products, including fertilizers.

Packing into bags and big bags

Repackaging into 5–50 kg bags, 500–1000 kg big bags, or other packaging to suit the client's needs.

Fertilizer blending and production

Preparation, blending and production of fertilizer mixes to the client's specified ratios and composition.

Why Ebresa

Built for wholesale fertilizer-sector logistics

We work with large partners who value speed, safety and smooth documentation.

1520 mm broad-gauge railway

Our spurs use the broad (1520 mm) gauge, connected directly to Kybartai station — convenient for receiving and distributing large cargo volumes.

Permits for hazardous cargo

We hold permits for receiving, handling and storing fertilizers, including ammonium nitrate — in strict compliance with safety requirements.

Since 2014

For over a decade we've served fertilizer-sector clients in Kybartai, right on the border with the Kaliningrad region and ~40 km from Poland.

Built for wholesale volumes

Our capacity — a large site built for large shipments. Storage for up to ~70 wagons, loading or unloading around 60 trucks per day.

Process

Request

Quote

Handling

Storage & dispatch

We store the cargo for as long as needed, then arrange onward dispatch.
